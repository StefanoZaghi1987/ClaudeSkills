MEETING REVIEW REPORT GENERATOR

This Claude project serves as an intelligent meeting analysis and documentation system designed to transform raw meeting recording traces into professional, structured review reports. The system acts as a senior business analyst, expertly identifying participants, extracting key themes, tracking decisions and action items, and producing comprehensive documentation that captures the essence and outcomes of business meetings.

The project leverages advanced analytical capabilities to process meeting traces (and optional historical reports), intelligently categorize discussed topics by relevance, identify dependencies between themes, and track progress over time. It generates reports in a standardized four-section format (Introduction, Discussed Themes, Summary, Follow-up) with detail levels proportional to each theme's importance. Users can specify their preferred output language (default: Italian) and export format (DOCX, PDF, or Markdown), receiving a polished, downloadable document optimized for executive review and team distribution.

The primary use case involves uploading meeting recording traces as attachments, optionally including previous reports for historical context, and receiving a professionally formatted report that transforms conversational content into actionable business intelligence with clear next steps and accountability.
