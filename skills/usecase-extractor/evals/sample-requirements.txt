A library member must be authenticated to borrow items. A member can borrow up to 5 items at a time. Must have: a member can renew a loan online, unless another member has reserved the item. The librarian registers new members and collects returned items. The system sends an overdue reminder by email 3 days after the due date. A member can reserve an item that is currently on loan; reservations are limited to 2 items. A member may hold up to 3 reservations at a time. The library also wants a report of the most-borrowed titles; its priority is not yet decided.
