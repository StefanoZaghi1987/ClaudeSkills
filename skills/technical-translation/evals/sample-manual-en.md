# Service card — Conveyor CV-15

## 1. Electrical inspection

**DANGER: Hazardous voltage. Contact with live parts causes death or serious injury. Disconnect the power supply and apply the lockout device before opening the control panel.**

The control panel is installed in accordance with EN 60204-1. Rated current: 16 A at 400 V three-phase.

**WARNING: Hot surfaces. The drive motor reaches 80 °C during operation. Allow it to cool before working on the belt.**

## 2. Belt replacement

1. Switch off the conveyor and wait for the complete stop.
2. Loosen the two M8 bolts and remove the guard.
3. Fit the new belt and tension it to 25 ± 0.5 Nm.

**CAUTION: Pinch hazard. Keep hands clear of the moving belt during operation.**

**NOTICE: Do not exceed the rated throughput of 6,000 pieces per hour; the gearbox may be damaged.**

## 3. Maintenance log

**NOTICE: Record the replacement date in the maintenance log. The next inspection is due on 12 March 2027.**
